AI Notes


inputs:
bird y coord, 
distance between bird and next top pipe,
distance between bird and next bottom pipe


outputs:
either jump or dont jump


activation function: tanh(x)
--the domain of tanh(x) is -infinity to infinity
--the range of the tanh(x) is -1 to 1
if the output of the tanh(x) function is greater than our set threshold then we tell the bird to jump, otherwise it doesnt jump.


POSSIBLE THRESHOLD: 0.5


Population size(how many birds will be running in each generation): 100 birds
The birds keep improving each generation


Fitness function: every x coordinate farther that a bird gets, the more points it scores


Max generations: 30
once we get past 30 generations, we should have found the perfect bird, if not then we have to try again from the beginning
